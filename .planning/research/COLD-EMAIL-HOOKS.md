## 2. TOP SUBJECT LINE FORMULAS FOR HANDLED PROSPECT OUTREACH

### Formula 1: The Curiosity Gap
Hint at insight without naming it.
```
"a thought about [business name]'s website"
"this might be a wild idea"
"something I noticed about [business name]"
```
**Why it works:** Forces the open to resolve the unknown. 46% open rate for question-style variants.

### Formula 2: The Quick Question
Frame as short, low-commitment question.
```
"quick question"
"question about [company name]"
"[first name], thoughts?"
```
**Why it works:** Tested across 150+ campaigns at 40%+ open rates consistently. Mimics internal email. "quick question" is one of the single highest-performing cold subject lines ever tested.

### Formula 3: The Specificity Hook
Reference a precise, researched detail about the prospect.
```
"your website on mobile"
"[company name]'s Google listing"
"the homepage at [company].com"
```
**Why it works:** Signals real research, not mass blast. Personalized subject lines get 46% open vs 35% generic.

### Formula 4: The Value-First Proposition
State a specific, quantified benefit.
```
"idea for [company name]'s website"
"3 things I'd change on [business].com"
"[first name] - a quick win for [company]"
```
**Why it works:** Numbers in subject lines can boost open rates by 113%.

### Formula 5: The Pattern Interrupt
Use a lowercase, internal-sounding phrase.
```
"hey"
"[first name]"
"thoughts?"
```
**Why it works:** Most effective for reaching senior decision-makers. Mimics a colleague's email, not marketing. "Hi [first name]" alone achieves 45.36% open rate.

### Formula 6: The "I Built This" Teaser
For spec work / pre-built prospect sites.
```
"this could be your new website"
"I redesigned [company name]'s homepage"
"made something for [company name]"
```
**Why it works:** Combines curiosity gap + specificity + value-first. Forces the open because they HAVE to see what you made.

## 3. OPENING LINE HOOKS (First 1-2 Sentences)

### The Science
- You have **6 seconds** to capture attention once the email is opened
- **58% of replies** come from the FIRST email, not follow-ups
- Personalized emails generate **6x higher transaction rates**
- Informal tone produces **78% higher positive reply rate** than formal
- **70% of B2B sales emails** still use generic openers = massive opportunity to stand out

### HOOKS THAT WORK (Ranked by Effectiveness)

#### Tier 1: Problem Callout (Highest Converting)
Point out a specific, real issue on their website.
```
"I was looking at [business].com on my phone and noticed [specific issue] --
thought you'd want to know."

"Pulled up [business name] on Google Maps and your site took 8 seconds to load.
That's costing you leads."

"I checked [business].com and noticed you don't have a way for customers
to book online -- that's leaving money on the table."
```
**Why:** Demonstrates real research. Creates urgency around a real problem. Web design agencies can easily identify specific issues (mobile responsiveness, load speed, no CTAs, outdated design, missing booking forms).

#### Tier 2: "I Built This For You" (Spec Work Hook)
Show, don't tell. The most powerful hook for agencies that pre-build prospect sites.
```
"I redesigned your homepage this morning -- wanted to show you what
[business name] could look like with a modern site."

"My team picked [business name] as our project of the week. We mocked up
a new version of your site -- want to see it?"

"Why waste your time telling you what I can do for you when I can
just show you?"
```
**Why:** Eliminates the biggest cold email barrier ("why should I care?"). The prospect literally CANNOT ignore a redesign of their own business. Lemlist data shows this template + personalized mockup image drives high engagement.

#### Tier 3: Competitor Intelligence
Reference what their competitors are doing.
```
"I noticed [competitor name] just launched a new website. Looks like
they're going after the same customers you are."

"Three [industry] businesses in [city] have redesigned their sites this year.
[Business name]'s site is starting to stand out -- and not in a good way."
```
**Why:** Loss aversion is 2x stronger than gain motivation. Nobody wants to fall behind competitors.

#### Tier 4: Genuine Compliment + Observation
```
"Love what you're doing at [business name] -- your [specific thing] is great.
Your website doesn't do it justice though."

"Found [business name] through [source]. The reviews are incredible --
but your site makes it hard for new customers to find you."
```
**Why:** The $29K cold email template (Folyo) uses exactly this structure: genuine compliment + bridge to their need. It works because it shows you actually looked at their business.

#### Tier 5: Stats-Driven Curiosity
```
"75% of people judge a business's credibility by their website.
I pulled up [business].com and..."

"Local businesses with modern websites get 2.5x more leads from Google.
Thought that might be relevant for [business name]."
```

### HOOKS TO AVOID
- "Hope this finds you well" -- signals zero research, instant delete
- "My name is X and I'm from Y" -- nobody cares who you are yet
- "I'd love to help you with..." -- too presumptuous, no earned credibility
- Any opener about YOU instead of THEM

## 4. THE "I BUILT THIS FOR YOU" PLAYBOOK (Spec Work Outreach)

### The Strategy
This is the highest-leverage play for Handled's prospect site outreach. Instead of describing what you COULD do, you show what you ALREADY DID.

### Template: The Lemlist "This Could Be Your New Website"
**Subject:** `this could be your new website`
**Body:**
```
Hi [first name],

Why waste time telling you what I can do when I can just show you?

My team evaluated [business name]'s current site and designed a modern
version that [specific benefit -- e.g., "makes it easy for customers
to book online" or "loads 3x faster on mobile"].

[Link to preview or screenshot]

If you like what you see, let's talk. If not, no worries -- it was
a fun project.

[Name]
```

### Template: The "Project of the Week" (Team Credibility)
**Subject:** `[business name] was our project this week`
**Body:**
```
Hi [first name],

Every week our team picks a local business and redesigns their site
as a creative exercise. This week, we picked [business name].

Here's what we came up with: [link]

We focused on [1-2 specific improvements -- mobile speed, online booking,
clearer service pages]. No strings attached -- but if you want to
make it live, we'd love to chat.

[Name]
```

### Template: The Problem-Solution (Audit + Build)
**Subject:** `3 things I'd fix on [business].com`
**Body:**
```
Hi [first name],

Pulled up [business name]'s website and noticed a few things that
might be costing you customers:

1. [Specific issue -- e.g., "No mobile-friendly booking form"]
2. [Specific issue -- e.g., "Homepage takes 6 seconds to load"]
3. [Specific issue -- e.g., "No Google reviews displayed on your site"]

I actually went ahead and built a version that fixes all three.
Want to take a look?

[Name]
```

### Why "I Built This" Works (The Psychology)
1. **Reciprocity** -- You gave them something valuable for free. They feel obligated to respond.
2. **Curiosity** -- They literally cannot resist seeing their own business redesigned.
3. **Proof over promise** -- No more "we could help you." It's "we already helped you."
4. **Effort signal** -- The time investment proves you're serious, not spamming.
5. **Loss aversion** -- Once they see the better version, they can't unsee it.

## 5. LOOM VIDEO STRATEGY (Permission-First)

### The Data
- Cold email + Loom video drives **19% higher reply rates** (Intercom data)
- Personalized video sees **16x higher click-to-open** rates
- **85% higher open rates** and **3.2x more responses** for video-based outreach
- One campaign: 3 clients at $2,500 each + one $7.5K contract = **$15K from one campaign**

### The Permission-First Framework
Do NOT embed video in the first email. Instead:

**Email 1:** Text-only cold email using hooks above. End with:
```
"Mind if I send a quick video walking through what I'd change?"
```

**Email 2 (only to those who reply):** Record a 60-90 second Loom:
- Open on their current website
- Deliver 3 specific recommendations
- Show the redesigned version you built
- Close with a specific meeting time

### Why Permission-First
- 1,000 emails sent --> ~14 replies --> 14 videos recorded (not 1,000)
- You invest recording time only in warm prospects
- The video itself = 20% of the outcome; targeting + deliverability = 80%

## 8. KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR HANDLED PROSPECT OUTREACH

1. **Subject lines:** Keep to 2-4 words, lowercase, question or curiosity format. "quick question" or "[business name]'s website" are proven performers.

2. **Opening hook:** Lead with a SPECIFIC problem you found on their site or show what you built. Never lead with who you are.

3. **The "I built this" angle is your superpower.** No other approach combines curiosity, reciprocity, proof, and personalization this effectively. This is what makes Handled's approach fundamentally different from every other web design cold email.

4. **Keep it under 100 words.** 75-100 words is the sweet spot. Every word must earn its place.

5. **CTA should be soft.** "Want to see it?" or "Mind if I share?" beats "Book a 15-minute call" every time.

6. **First email matters most.** 55-58% of replies come from email #1. Make it count.

7. **Informal > formal.** 78% higher positive reply rate. Write like a human, not an agency.

8. **Follow up 3 times** over 14 days, but only send video to people who respond.
